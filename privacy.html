Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026. Silver Construction LLC ("we") provides bathroom remodeling estimates at this website.

What we collect

When you request an estimate, we collect the name, email address, phone number, bathroom zip code, and any notes you enter. We also keep the package and options you selected so we can prepare your estimate.

How we use it

We use your information to send your estimate, to follow up about your project, and, only if you check the consent box on the estimate form, to contact you by phone, email, and text message about your estimate and related offers.

Consent

Agreeing to be contacted is optional and is not a condition of getting an estimate. If you agree, you may reply STOP to any text message to end texts, or ask us by phone or email to stop calling or emailing you. Message and data rates may apply to texts.

Sharing

We do not sell your information and do not share your phone number or email address with third parties for their marketing. We use service providers that handle sending email and hosting this website, and they process your information only to provide those services.

Keeping your information

We keep estimate requests for as long as needed to serve your project and meet legal record-keeping requirements.
